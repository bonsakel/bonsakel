## About me

I like turning messy data into something people can use, whether that is a web app, a map, a dashboard, or an AI tool. I've built software for healthcare, pharmacy, and data startups, and I'm happiest when what I build ends up in front of real people.

- 🎓 Senior at [Furman University](https://furman.edu), studying **Computer Science** with a minor in **Data Analytics**
- 🗺️ Into **GIS and spatial analysis**, and into finding patterns in data by place
- 🤖 Building with **LLMs and generative AI**, from AI inboxes to recommendation systems
- 🧑‍🏫 Former teaching assistant. I love helping other people learn to code
- 🎶 Outside of tech, I'm into music, and I enjoy exploring ideas that make an impact

**Open to:** software engineering, data, and AI roles.
